About

At West Window Picture House we make movies, tv series, social content, music videos and ads. We are dedicated to crafting elevated content and creating cinematic experiences that captivate and inspire.

Founded by Alexandria E. Martin. A Black female producer and filmmaker with a rich background in working with industry leaders such as Annapurna and STX, and a portfolio of award-winning short films, we bring a unique and authentic voice to the promotional content space.

Our mission is to blend the artistry and introspection while being insightful and impactful storytellers. We specialize in producing branded content, commercials, and social media spots that are not only promotional but also profoundly cinematic.

Driven by a passion for representation, West Window Picture House is committed to showcasing a broad spectrum of voices and perspectives. From conceptualization to final delivery, we aim to break barriers and challenge conventions in storytelling, ensuring every project we undertake is both compelling and meaningful.

Join us as we define for ourselves a new landscape of promotional content, one cinematic experience at a time.

What is the Meaning Behind West Window Picture House?

West Window Picture House draws its name from the evocative imagery of a western-facing window, a vantage point renowned for capturing the most breathtaking sunsets. Just as a western-facing window frames the end of the day in a golden light, it also serves as a point for reflection, capturing the themes and essence of the day much like cinema does for stories.

A window is more than just a pane of glass; it is a prophetic portal into one's perspective, reality, and life. West Window Picture House thus becomes a home of artistic vision, a fact that underscores its promise of illuminating stories that provide the lens to connect us all.
